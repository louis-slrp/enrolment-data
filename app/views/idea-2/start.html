More Timely Data Pilot

This service is for colleges to submit and view weekly enrolment data.

Use this service to:

  • submit enrolment data
  • view submitted enrolment data

Before you start

You must submit enrolment data every week for learners at your college who:

  • are enrolled on at least one learning aim
  • are receiving 16 to 19 funding (FundModel = 25)

Do not include learners who have:

  • completed all learning aims
  • withdrawn from all learning aims

You will need to upload a CSV file containing the Unique Learner Numbers (ULNs) for eligible learners.
